[MEDIUM] Deep synchronous call chain (Reliability; authored)
  4 blocking hops in a row (users → cdn → lb → app → db) with no circuit breaker or asynchronous step. A slow or failed component at the end stalls every caller.
  Fix: Add circuit breakers with timeouts along the chain, or move a step behind a queue or event stream.

[LOW] Stateful component without recovery objectives (Operational Excellence; authored)
  PostgreSQL holds data but has no recovery point (RPO) or recovery time (RTO) objective.
  Fix: Set dr.rpo_sec and dr.rto_sec for PostgreSQL and confirm the backup strategy meets them.
